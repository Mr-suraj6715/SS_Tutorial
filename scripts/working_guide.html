📋 Overview: Is document me SS Tutorial Coaching Website & Management System ki poori functioning, architecture aur sabhi 5 User Roles (Visitor, Student, Parent, Teacher, Admin) ke adhikaar (permissions) aur daily workflows ko vistaar se explain kiya gaya hai.

1. Har User Role Ka Kaam (Who Can Do What)

1. Public Visitor (Bina Login Ke Koi Bhi)
Open Access
Naye students, parents ya visitors jo coaching ke baare me jankari lene website par aate hain.
  • Home Page Explore Karna: Coaching ka introduction, Ankit Sir (Director) ki profile, batch schedule aur reviews dekhna.
  • Courses & Syllabus Check Karna: Class 6th se 12th tak CBSE aur SSC boards ke courses, subject details aur batch timings dekhna.
  • Online Admission Form Bharna: Student details aur required documents upload karke admission ke liye apply karna (status: Pending review).
  • Direct WhatsApp Chat: Floating WhatsApp button se direct +91 7738820436 par judna.
  • Toppers & Results Dekhna: Pichhle saalo ke board exam toppers, high score marks aur awards dekhna.
  • Campus Photo Gallery & Video Lectures: Classrooms ki photos, YouTube videos aur Instagram reels dekhna.
  • Inquiry Form Send Karna: Contact page par message bhejna jo turant institute ke Admin ke paas jata hai.
2. Student Portal (Enrolled Vidyarthi)
Student Login
Enrolled student apne registered email aur password se login karke apna portal access karta hai.
  • Personalized Dashboard: Enrolled batch, course name aur profile details dekhna.
  • Attendance Tracker: Roz ki attendance — kitne din Present, Absent ya Late rahe aur total attendance percentage (%).
  • Exam Results & Scorecard: Weekly tests, unit tests aur preliminary exams me kitne marks mile aur passing marks kya the dekhna.
  • Study Material Download: Faculty dwara upload kiye gaye chapter notes, formula cheatsheets aur question papers download karna.
  • Fees & Balance Status: Total course fees, jama ki gayi fees aur bacha hua pending balance dekhna.
3. Parent Portal (Abhibhavak)
Parent Login
Parents apne bachhe ki padhai, attendance aur fee details par ghar baithe nazar rakh sakte hain.
  • Multiple Child Selector: Agar ek se zyada bachhe coaching me padhte hain, toh unhe dropdown se switch karke dekhna.
  • Daily Attendance Report: Bachha aaj coaching gaya ya nahi (Present / Absent / Late) date-wise check karna.
  • Test Marks & Progress: Har test ke evaluated marks aur overall academic growth monitor karna.
  • Fees Status & Receipts: Fees payment history aur aage ki due date check karna.
4. Teacher / Faculty Portal (Shikshak)
Faculty Login
Teachers apne assigned batches aur students ki daily academic activities manage karte hain.
  • Assigned Batches View: Unhe kaun sa batch padhana hai aur unme kitne students registered hain dekhna.
  • Daily Attendance Entry: Apne batch ke students ko single-click me Present / Absent / Late mark karna aur submit karna.
  • Upload Study Materials: PDF notes, formula sheets aur assignment files upload karna jo enrolled students ko turant mil jati hain.
  • Enter Exam Marks: Conduct kiye gaye test ke liye sabhi students ke marks enter aur save karna.
5. Admin Command Center (Director / Main Administrator)
Super Admin Access
Institute Director ke paas website, users, academic records aur settings ka 100% full control hota hai.
  • Command Center (Overview): Real-time total students, pending admissions, unread inquiries aur revenue stats dekhna.
  • Admissions Manager: Online form se aayi hui nayi applications review karna, unhe Approve ya Reject karna aur batch assign karna.
  • Users & Roles Manager: Naye Teachers, Students ya Parents create karna, passwords reset karna aur role privileges change karna.
  • Courses & Subjects Manager: Naye courses banana, syllabus likhna, subjects define karna aur course fee decide karna.
  • Batches Manager: Naye morning/evening batches create karna, timing schedule set karna, teacher assign karna aur seat capacity limit lagana.
  • Fees & Dues Management: Invoices create karna, offline/online aayi hui fees ko Paid mark karna aur pending balance track karna.
  • Exams & Toppers: Naye exams create karna (Total Marks, Passing Marks), results publish karna aur toppers ko website par highlight karna.
  • Website Content Management: Gallery me naye photos upload karna, blog articles publish karna, YouTube aur Instagram Reels link add karna, testimonials approve karna.
  • Institute Settings: Institute name, logo, official phone number (7738820436), notice ticker, maps embed aur admin credentials update karna.
  • Live Inquiries Inbox: Website contact form se aayi sabhi enquiries ko real-time dekhna aur status update karna.

3. Coaching Ka Daily Operational Workflow

📌 Admission Workflow (Kaise Hota Hai):
1. Parent/Student website ke /admission page par jaakar online form bharte hain.
2. Form submit hote hi Admin ke dashboard me Real-Time SSE Alert aata hai.
3. Admin application review karta hai aur use "Approved" mark karke specific Class/Batch me allot kar deta hai.
4. Student ka account active ho jata hai aur student portal access mil jata hai.
📌 Daily Class & Attendance Workflow:
1. Teacher coaching aakar Teacher Portal login karta hai aur apna Batch select karta hai.
2. Har student ko single click me Present/Absent/Late mark karke "Save Attendance" dabata hai.
3. Student aur Parent ke Portal par attendance turant update ho jati hai.
📌 Exams & Study Materials Workflow:
1. Admin ya Teacher naya test schedule karte hain (e.g. "Maths Chapter 1 Unit Test").
2. Test conduct hone ke baad teacher marks portal me enter kar deta hai.
3. Student aur Parent apne login me marks, percentage aur remarks dekh sakte hain.
